    <div class="grid" id="analysis">
        <section class="card pro">
            <h2>The Pros</h2>
            <ul>
                <li><strong>Flexibility:</strong> Learn at your own pace and on your own schedule.</li>
                <li><strong>Accessibility:</strong> Students can access world-class resources from anywhere.</li>
                <li><strong>Cost-Effective:</strong> Eliminates travel costs and expensive physical textbooks.</li>
            </ul>
        </section>

        <section class="card con">
            <h2>The Cons</h2>
            <ul>
                <li><strong>Digital Divide:</strong> Not every student has equal access to high-speed internet or hardware.</li>
                <li><strong>Self-Discipline:</strong> Without a teacher present, it's easy to get distracted by Discord or games.</li>
                <li><strong>Isolation:</strong> Lack of face-to-face social interaction with peers.</li>
            </ul>
        </section>
    </div>

    <section id="conclusion" class="card">
        <h2>Final Verdict</h2>
        <p>E-learning is a powerful tool, but it works best when combined with traditional methods. As technology improves, the focus must remain on making learning accessible for everyone, regardless of their background.</p>
